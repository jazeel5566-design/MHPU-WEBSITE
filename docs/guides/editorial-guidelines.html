Editorial guidelines for mhpu.org

How to write, approve and publish articles, notices and documents on the MHPU website, in English and Dhivehi.

ForEveryone on the editorial team: Authors, Editors, the General Secretary and the President CoversEditorial portal: articles, documents, campaigns, events, media library, Dhivehi versions Version1.0 · November 2026

Sign-off rule
Statements and press releases speak for the union. They must be approved by the General Secretary or the President before an Editor publishes them. Routine news and member notices only need an Editor.

2How an article moves from draft to live

DraftOnly the team can see it. Save as often as you like: Save draft.
In reviewAuthor clicks Submit for review. It appears in the Editors' Review queue.
PublishedEditor clicks Publish now, or chooses a date and time and clicks Schedule.
Live changesEditors edit and click Update live article. Unpublish takes it offline. Archive retires it.

If an article needs changes, the Editor uses Return to author with a note. The Author sees the note, fixes the article and submits it again.

3Writing an article

  1. Editorial portal → Write new article.
  2. Headline: clear and specific, under about 90 characters. Say what happened, not just the topic. "MHPU wins Ramadan allowance for essential staff", not "Ramadan update".
  3. Summary (standfirst): one or two sentences shown on news cards and in search results. It should make sense on its own.
  4. Body: use the toolbar for headings, bold, lists, links and quotes. Use Preview to check. Keep paragraphs short: two to four sentences.
  5. Section: News, Press release, Statement, Member notice or Feature story.
  6. Who can read it? Everyone (public), or Members only. Members-only articles show a short teaser to the public and ask visitors to log in or join.
  7. Cover image: a landscape photo, at least 1200 px wide. See section 8 on photos and consent.
  8. Tags (optional) and Web address: the address is made from the headline automatically. Keep it short and don't change it after publishing, or links already shared will break.
  9. Feature on homepage (Editors only): use it for the one or two most important stories.
  10. Add the Dhivehi version (section 4), then Submit for review (Authors) or Publish now (Editors).

4Dhivehi versions

The website has a full Dhivehi version. Visitors switch with the ދިވެހި / EN button at the top of every page. Menus, buttons and page text switch automatically. Your content switches only if you add a Dhivehi version. Otherwise the English is shown.

  • Articles: in the editor, click the ދިވެހި tab and fill in the title, summary and body. The editor writes right-to-left in Faruma automatically.
  • Campaigns: fill in Title (ދިވެހި) and Summary (ދިވެހި).
  • Events: fill in the Dhivehi title.
  • Workplace unions (Admin): Name (ދިވެހި).
  • Site banner (Admin → Site settings): Banner text in Dhivehi.
Dhivehi standards
  • Translate the meaning, not word for word. Use formal, respectful Dhivehi suitable for an official union.
  • Keep names of hospitals, laws and people as they are officially written. Where an English name is better known, use it in Thaana script with the English in brackets the first time if helpful.
  • Write numbers, dates and amounts the same way as in English (e.g. MVR 100, 24 November 2026, 119).
  • A second Dhivehi speaker should check every statement and press release before it is published.
  • If there's no time to translate, publish in English and add the Dhivehi version later. Never put machine translation online unchecked.

5Publishing documents

Documents (agreements, guides, forms, reports, the constitution) are published in the Resources library at mhpu.org/resources. Only Editors and above can upload them.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the file (see the checklist below), then go to Editorial portal → Documents.
  2. Title: what it is, plus the year or version if relevant. "Know your rights: overtime pay (2026)", "2027 pay claim: draft for consultation".
  3. Description: one sentence on what it's for and who it's for.
  4. Category: Agreements · Policy & positions · Know your rights · Forms · Reports & research · Constitution & rules.
  5. Who can download it? Everyone, or Members only (members must log in to download).
  6. File: PDF, Word or Excel, up to 4 MB. Upload and check it appears correctly under Resources.

Choosing "Everyone" or "Members only"

Everyone (public)Members onlyNever upload
Know-your-rights guides, the constitution, published statements and reports, public forms (e.g. the membership form), signed agreements once announced publicly. Drafts for consultation, negotiation positions, internal reports, rep handbooks, AGM papers, anything the ExCom decides is for members. Anything with a member's or patient's personal details (names with ID numbers, phone numbers, medical information, casework), unredacted employer correspondence, passwords or bank details, documents the union doesn't have the right to share.
"Members only" protects downloads, not copies
Any member can download and forward a members-only document. If a document must not leave a small group, don't upload it to the website. Share it directly instead.

Preparing the file

  • PDF is preferred for anything people only read. Use Word or Excel only for forms people fill in.
  • Export from Word or Google Docs as PDF rather than scanning, so the text can be searched and read by screen readers. If you must scan, use a clear, straight, high-contrast scan.
  • Keep it under 4 MB. If it's larger, use "Reduce file size" or "Optimise PDF", lower the image quality, or split it into parts.
  • Name the file clearly before uploading, e.g. MHPU-overtime-guide-2026.pdf. No spaces or special characters is best.
  • Remove tracked changes, comments and hidden personal information (File → Info → Inspect document in Word).
  • Check the document's own date, version and logo are correct, and that it uses the official letterhead where appropriate.
  • Add a Dhivehi version as a separate document where one exists, with "(ދިވެހި)" in the title.

Updating or removing a document

An uploaded file can't be edited. To publish a new version, upload the new file first, check it, then delete the old one, so there's never a gap. You can change Who can download it? at any time without re-uploading. Delete documents that are out of date. An old agreement or rate can mislead members.

The website checks every upload. Files that aren't really PDF, Word, Excel or image files are refused, even if they're renamed.

6Campaigns and petitions

  • Campaign title and Summary: short, and focused on the outcome you want.
  • The demand people sign: one clear sentence. Signers agree to exactly this, so get it approved (section 1 sign-off rule).
  • Signature goal: realistic. A goal you can beat is better than one you never reach.
  • Status: Draft → Active → Won or Closed. When a campaign is won, change the status to Won and publish a news article about the result.
  • Feature in homepage hero: only one campaign at a time.
  • Signers' details are personal data. They're visible only to staff and used only for that campaign, as the form promises.

7Events and online meetings

  • Choose the Type (Meeting, Training, CPD, Rally, AGM, Webinar), the Audience (public or members only), start and end times, and a location.
  • For CPD sessions, enter the CPD hours. Set a Capacity if places are limited.
  • For online meetings, tick Online event and paste the Google Meet link. Members see a Join button in the member portal from 15 minutes before the start until the meeting ends. The link itself is never shown to the public.
  • Untick Published (visible) to prepare an event before announcing it.

8Photos and the media library

  • Upload images (JPG, PNG, WebP; max 4 MB each) in Media library, then copy the link into an article or campaign.
  • Always write Alt text: a short description of what the photo shows, for people using screen readers. "Nurses at the MHPU branch meeting in Malé", not "photo1".
  • Consent: get permission from anyone clearly identifiable before publishing their photo. Never publish photos of patients, patient records, screens showing patient data, or the inside of wards where patients can be identified.
  • Use our own photos or properly licensed ones. Don't copy images from other websites or social media.
  • Site-wide photos (home page, page headers) are changed by the Admin under Site photos.

10Accuracy, privacy and the law

  • Check facts: figures, dates, names and quotes. Link to or keep the source.
  • Privacy: never name a member involved in a case, disciplinary matter or dispute without their written agreement. Never publish patient information of any kind.
  • Fairness: don't make allegations against a named person or employer that we can't prove. If in doubt, ask the General Secretary before publishing.
  • Embargoes: use Schedule for news that must not go out before a set time.
  • Copyright: don't republish other organisations' articles in full. Summarise and link.
  • Corrections: fix mistakes quickly with Update live article. For a mistake of substance, add a line at the end: "Corrected on [date]: [what changed]." Use Unpublish only for serious problems, and tell the General Secretary.

11Keeping the website secure

  • Never share your login. Use a password of at least 10 characters that you don't use anywhere else.
  • Log out on shared computers.
  • Only paste ordinary web links (https://…). The website automatically disables unsafe links.
  • Be wary of emails asking you to log in through a link. Always go to mhpu.org/login yourself.
  • Tell the Admin immediately if you think your account has been used by someone else.

12Checklists

Before submitting or publishing an article

  • Headline clear and specific
  • Summary written
  • Facts, names, figures and dates checked
  • Correct section and audience (public / members only)
  • Cover image with permission and alt text
  • Links work
  • Dhivehi version added, or planned
  • Statements and press releases approved by the GS or President
  • Previewed on a phone

Before uploading a document

  • Right document, latest version
  • No personal or patient data, comments or tracked changes
  • PDF (unless it's a fillable form), under 4 MB
  • Clear file name and title, with year or version
  • Correct category
  • Public or members only decided
  • Old version deleted after the new one is checked
