Elections and ballots on mhpu.org

How online voting works, how to run a workplace union ExCom election or a member ballot, and how the website keeps every vote secret.

ForMHPU ExCom, the website admin, workplace union officers and election officials CoversAdmin portal → Elections, Admin portal → Ballots, Member portal → Elections / Ballots & votes Version1.0 · November 2026

Who can set them up
Only the Admin role can create and manage elections and ballots. Officers (President, General Secretary, Treasurer) can vote as members but can't change an election. Every action is recorded in the Audit log.

2The life of an election

Every election moves through three stages, decided only by the opening and closing times you set.

UpcomingBefore the opening time. Positions and candidates can be added, changed or removed. Members can see the candidates but can't vote.
OpenBetween the opening and closing times. Eligible members vote. Everything is locked: no candidate or position can be changed. No running totals are shown, only turnout.
ClosedAfter the closing time. All votes are counted. Admins see the results and decide when to publish them to members.

3Running a workplace union ExCom election

Before you start

  • Follow the workplace union's own constitution for notice, nominations and eligibility. The website runs the vote. Nominations, objections and the election committee happen as your rules require.
  • Make sure the union exists under Admin → Workplace unions and is marked Active, and that its members are assigned to it (each member's record shows their workplace union).
  • Decide whether members must have paid-up dues to vote. If so, remind members early: the dues reminders and online payment make this easy.

Step by step

  1. Create the election. Admin portal → Elections → fill in:
    • Election title, e.g. "Dharumavantha Union: ExCom election 2027".
    • Who votes? Pick the workplace union, or "All MHPU members" for a federation-wide election.
    • Voting opens and Voting closes (Maldives time).
    • Information for voters: term length, how nominations were held, who to contact with questions.
    • Only members whose dues are up to date can vote: ticked by default.
  2. Add the positions, e.g. President (1 seat), Vice President (1), Secretary (1), Treasurer (1), Committee members (3 seats). "Seats" is how many people are elected to that position, and therefore how many a voter may choose.
  3. Add the candidates to each position by membership number (e.g. MHPU-0123), with an optional short statement shown on the ballot. The website checks that the candidate is an active member and, for a workplace union election, a member of that union.
  4. Check everything on the election page: spelling of names, positions, seats, times. Ask a second officer to check too.
    Once voting opens, the election is locked
    Positions and candidates can't be changed after the opening time, so a vote can't be altered while it's running. If you find a mistake before opening, fix it. If you find it after, the election committee must decide what to do (for example, delete the election before anyone votes and create a new one).
  5. Tell members. Publish a member notice (Editorial portal), send an SMS (Admin → SMS), and mention it at meetings. Members find it in the member portal under Elections. A red badge shows on their menu while an election is open and they haven't voted.
  6. While voting is open, the election page shows Votes cast and Turnout (as a percentage of eligible members). Use turnout to decide whether to send a reminder. Vote counts per candidate are hidden from everyone, including admins.
  7. After closing, open the election page. The results appear immediately: candidates ranked by votes, with winners marked by a trophy. If there's a tie at the cut-off (e.g. two candidates level for the last seat), it's flagged. Resolve it as your union rules require (a run-off, a draw, etc.).
  8. Publish the results. Click Publish results. Members then see the full results in the portal. You can unpublish if needed.

4What members see and do

  1. Log in at mhpu.org/login → Elections → open the election.
  2. If they can't vote, the page tells them why in plain words: not an active member, not in this workplace union, or dues not up to date ("Pay your dues, then come back to vote").
  3. For each position, tap the candidates they want, up to the number of seats. Leaving a position empty counts as an abstention for that position.
  4. Tap Review my choices, check the summary, then Submit my vote. A vote can't be changed after it's submitted.
  5. They see a confirmation, and the election shows "You voted on [date and time]". Trying to vote again is refused.

Members vote on a phone or computer. No app is needed. Members who can't vote online can be helped by the office as your rules allow, but staff must never vote on a member's behalf or ask for their password.

5Running a ballot (a yes/no or multiple-choice question)

  1. Admin portal → Ballots → Create a ballot.
  2. Enter a title, the exact question, an optional description (link to the proposal or agreement), the options one per line (e.g. "Yes, accept the offer" / "No, reject the offer"), and the opening and closing times.
  3. Members see it under Ballots & votes when it opens, choose one option and confirm. A red banner on their dashboard reminds them while it's open.
  4. While it's open, the admin page shows only how many have voted and turnout. When it closes, the totals and percentages appear for admins and members.
Check ballots carefully before they open
Ballots can't be edited after creation. If there's a mistake, delete the ballot (which also deletes any votes cast) and create it again, ideally before it opens. Deleting a ballot with votes needs a clear decision by the ExCom.

6How the website keeps votes secret

A secret ballot means nobody can find out how a particular member voted. That includes ExCom members, admins, the website developer and anyone with access to the database. This is how the website achieves it.

Two separate records

1 · Who has voted
The member's name and the time they voted. Used to stop double voting, show "You voted" and calculate turnout. It contains no choices.
2 · The votes
Just the choices: which candidate or option. Each vote has no name, no membership number and no time, so it can't be matched to a voter.

Sealed, then counted in shuffled batches

Saving a voter's name and their vote at the same moment could, in theory, let a database expert link the two by their timing or order. To prevent that:

  1. When a member votes, the website records that they voted and stores their choices in a sealed (encrypted) envelope. The envelope holds no name and can't be read without the website's secret key.
  2. Envelopes are only opened and counted in batches of at least five, or all together when voting closes. The choices in a batch are shuffled and saved as anonymous votes in one go, and the envelopes are destroyed.
  3. The website checks for elections and ballots that have closed every morning and when results are viewed, so every vote is counted.

No running totals

Nobody, including admins, can see how candidates or options are doing while voting is open. This stops early results from influencing voters and stops anyone working out how a recent voter voted by watching the numbers change.

What admins can see

  • Turnout: how many have voted and the percentage of eligible members.
  • Who has voted and when, stored in the database for checking eligibility and disputes. Admins don't see this list on screen, and it never shows choices.
  • The final results after closing.
The limits of any online vote
  • Very small electorates. If only a handful of people vote and the result is unanimous, the result itself reveals how everyone voted. No system can prevent that. For tiny groups, consider whether a secret vote makes sense.
  • Shared passwords. The website can't tell if someone logs in with another member's password. Remind members never to share their password. Staff must never ask for it.
  • Keep the secret key safe. The envelope key is a website setting (SESSION_SECRET) kept in Vercel. It must not be changed while a vote is open, or sealed votes can't be counted.

7Checklist for the election committee

Before voting opens

  • Notice given as the union constitution requires
  • Nominations closed and candidates confirmed
  • Workplace union and its members correct on the website
  • Dues rule decided and announced
  • Election created, positions and seats correct
  • Every candidate added, names checked by a second officer
  • Opening and closing times checked (Maldives time)
  • Members notified: notice, SMS, meetings

During and after

  • Turnout watched, reminder sent if needed
  • Questions from members answered (eligibility, dues)
  • Results reviewed by the committee after closing
  • Any tie resolved under the rules
  • Results published to members
  • Result announced: notice, SMS, meeting
  • Outcome recorded in the committee's minutes

8Questions and problems

SituationWhat to do
A member says they can't voteThe election page tells them why. Usually their dues aren't up to date, they're in a different workplace union, or their membership isn't active. Check their record in Admin → Members.
A member is in the wrong workplace unionCorrect it on their member record before voting opens. Changing union memberships during a vote should be decided by the election committee.
A member wants to change their voteNot possible. Votes can't be changed or withdrawn after submitting, and the website can't find which vote was theirs.
A candidate withdrawsBefore opening: remove them from the position. After opening: the election is locked. The committee decides under the rules, and the result can be read accordingly.
Wrong closing timeCan be edited only while the election is upcoming. After it opens, decide with the committee.
Can an election be deleted?Only if nobody has voted yet. After that, it stays on record.
Will results appear if nobody opens the page?Yes. The website counts all sealed votes of closed elections and ballots each morning automatically.
Can we turn voting off?Yes. Admin → Pages & features can switch off Elections and/or Ballots for members and admins when they aren't needed.

9A short explanation for members

You can copy this into a notice or SMS:

Your vote is secret. When you vote on mhpu.org, the union records only that you have voted, so you can't vote twice. Your choices are sealed and counted together with other members' votes, with no name or time attached. Nobody, including union officials, can see how you voted, and no one sees any results until voting closes. Log in at mhpu.org/login, open Elections (or Ballots & votes), make your choices and press Submit. Never share your password.
